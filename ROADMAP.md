# Research roadmap

Priorities, not commitments or claims of completion.

1. Confirm licensing rights and complete the public repository settings.
2. Recover the exact nine-test source and missing J1/J4/J5 findings; independently review the latest eleven-test model.
3. Evaluate the ten-point prompt on synthetic, benign tasks and adversarial persuasion scenarios from authorised peers and external content; measure attempts and effects separately.
4. Design a genuinely separate host enforcement boundary and explicit task/operation/resource grants, with protected identities and logs, scoped sharing, collective limits and containment.
5. Run T01–T37 in controlled synthetic environments, with documented negative controls, reproducibility and legitimate-work regressions.
6. Obtain independent implementation review, address residual risks and publish versioned evidence. Do not infer deployment readiness from fixture tests.

Contributions from non-programmers, developers, researchers and governance practitioners are welcome. Use public issues for non-sensitive proposals and the security policy for sensitive findings.
