# Code of conduct

We welcome rigorous criticism of ideas, specifications, tests and evidence. Treat contributors respectfully; distinguish evidence from opinion and disclose relevant conflicts. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, disclosure of secrets, spam and intentional misrepresentation of security evidence are unacceptable.

Raise conduct concerns privately with the project owner, @amechi-ai, through an agreed private channel. The owner may remove harmful content, warn or restrict participation proportionately and explain decisions where privacy permits. Retaliation against good-faith reporting is prohibited. Contributors may request reconsideration. This policy applies to project issues, discussions and pull requests.
